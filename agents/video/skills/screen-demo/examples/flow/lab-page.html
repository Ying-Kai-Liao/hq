What the clips are built from

HyperFrames + a camera rig

Each take is an HTML composition rendered frame by frame. The camera is one transform, so callouts and the cursor move with the footage. A move lasts 0.35 s plus 1.35 ms per pixel travelled, between 0.6 and 2.4 s (the rule comes from Cinetic).

Your brand, not a template

Ink, paper and Inter, from the flow brand. Green appears only where there's a decision, the same job the crossbar does in the mark.

Zoom limit

Zooms stop at 1.7× of the retina source, so terminal text stays readable. In round 1 the zoom targets are placed by hand. Next round they come from your clicks (Cap) or from detecting where the screen changes.

Render speed

12–23 s per clip on the Mac mini, at 1080p and 30 fps. A 90 s demo should take about 3–4 minutes.
