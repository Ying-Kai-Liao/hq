# flow — video design

Derived from the approved flow brand (`~/Workspace/flow/docs/brand/README.md`, 2026-10-12) and the user's taste
(`hq/shared/user.md`).

## Style Prompt
Quiet, precise, editorial. Real terminal footage is the hero; the frame around it stays calm. Colour is saved for
the one meaningful thing: the moment a decision is needed or made, which is what the green crossbar means in the
mark. Motion is a camera, not effects: it moves to where the action is, holds, and moves on.

## Colors
- Ink `#15171C`: dark stage, text on light
- Paper `#EEEDE8`: light stage (warm off-white, light grain)
- White `#FFFFFF`: text on ink
- Decision green `#0B9A68` on light, `#2FD18F` on ink: only for decisions (questions, answers, the crossbar)
- Muted `#8A8F98`: secondary labels

## Typography
- Inter (400/600/700) for all supporting text. Always lowercase "flow"; never type the wordmark, use the SVG.
- JetBrains Mono only when quoting terminal text.

## What NOT to Do
- No green on anything that isn't a decision.
- No gradients, glows, neon, node-dot graphs or stacked waves (brand don'ts + user dislikes).
- No shadows/outlines on the logo (window shadows are fine).
- Don't zoom more than ~2.6× into terminal text (it blurs); never cut mid-camera-move.
- No more than 6 words per caption line.
