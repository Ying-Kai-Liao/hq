## 2.4.0

### zh-Hant

- 當 iPhone App 和電腦上的 Codync 版本無法搭配時，會清楚告訴你要更新哪一邊，並提供更新按鈕。
- 電腦更新完成後，App 會自動重新連線並恢復同步。
- 在 iPad 遠端畫面上，可以用外接鍵盤以注音等輸入法打中文，也能用 Ctrl-Space 切換輸入法。

### en-US

- When this app and Codync on your computer can't work together, Codync now tells you which one to update and offers the update.
- After your computer updates, the app reconnects and syncs again on its own.
- On iPad remote screen, a hardware keyboard now types Chinese and other IME input, and Ctrl-Space switches input sources.
