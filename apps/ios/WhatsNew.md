## 2.8.0

### zh-Hant

- Bot 改成像 Grok 一樣傳訊息：工作途中一小段一小段發給你，每則都是完整的訊息，不再逐字跳動。
- Bot 正在寫訊息時會顯示「Typing…」；新訊息會輕輕彈入。
- 對話捲動與送出訊息更順暢，「思考中」的狀態不會再被輸入框擋住。
- 輸入框不再跳出「貼上圖片」提示；要貼上圖片請用「+」選單裡的「貼上」。
- Live Activity 與動態島更生動：bot 的表情會跟著狀態變，需要你時文字轉成琥珀色並出現「Review」按鈕，工作時間會自己往上數。
- 新增「Team」小工具：用 2×2 角色格一眼看四個 bot，需要你的排在最前面。
- Bots 小工具每一列右側直接顯示上色的狀態。
- 新增控制中心按鈕：顯示最需要你的 bot，一點就打開。

### en-US

- Bots now message you the way Grok does: short, complete messages as they work, instead of text typing itself out.
- You see "Typing…" while a bot writes, and new messages pop in gently.
- Scrolling and sending feel smoother, and the thinking indicator no longer hides behind the message box.
- The message box no longer pops up a "Paste image" chip; use Paste in the + menu instead.
- Livelier Live Activities and Dynamic Island: the bot's face follows its state, the text turns amber with a Review button when it needs you, and the elapsed time counts up on its own.
- New Team widget: four bots as characters in a 2×2 grid, the ones that need you first.
- Each row of the Bots widget shows its state in color.
- New Control Center control: the bot that needs you most, one tap to open it.
